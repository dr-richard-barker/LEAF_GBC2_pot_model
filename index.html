4. Mathematical Physics & CFD Transport Governing Equations

Full 3D Navier–Stokes, Brinkman–Darcy porous media drag, Churchill mixed convection, and coupled Farquhar–von Caemmerer–Berry (FvCB) photosynthesis formulations.

1. Incompressible Navier–Stokes with Brinkman Porous Drag & Boussinesq Buoyancy

∇ · u = 0
∂u/∂t + (u · ∇)u = -(1/ρ₀)∇p + ν∇²u - D(x)u + g[βT(T - T₀) + βH₂O(CH₂O - CH₂O,0) + βCO₂(CCO₂ - CCO₂,0)]

Continuity and momentum balance where ν = 1.51 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s. The momentum sink D(x) equals Darcy drag ν / (εK) inside the 3 partitioned substrate blocks (Y = 7.62–14.62 mm) and canopy drag Cd · aleaf(x) · |u| in foliar zones. Buoyancy acceleration drops 83.4% on the Moon (g = 1.62 m/s²).

2. Multi-Species Advection–Diffusion & Sandberg Residence-Time Identity

∂Ck/∂t + u · ∇Ck = Dk ∇²Ck + Sk(x)
u · ∇θ - Dθ ∇²θ = 1,   θ|Port 1 = 0
⟨θ⟩outlet, flux = ∫Port 2 (u · n)θ dA / ∫Port 2 (u · n) dA ≡ τnominal = Vfluid / Q

Scalar transport for CO₂ (D = 1.60 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s) and H₂O vapor (D = 2.42 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s). The Mean Age-of-Air (θ) PDE verifies ventilation efficiency, validated against the exact Sandberg conservation ratio (θout / τ = 0.938).

3. Churchill Combined Free-and-Forced Leaf Boundary Layer Conductance

δeff,c = (δforced,c⁻³ + δnat,c⁻³)-1/3
δforced,c = 4.92 √(ν Lleaf,c / |uc|),   δnat,c = 3.5 Lleaf,c Grc-1/4
gbl,c = DCO₂ / (δeff,c · Vmol)

Churchill asymptotic blending across laminar forced Blasius flat-plate boundary layers and natural Grashof buoyancy convection. Evaluated with Lleaf = 20 mm for B. rapa and 8 mm for Arabidopsis.

4. Coupled Farquhar–von Caemmerer–Berry (FvCB) Photosynthesis & Stomatal Supply

Anet = min(Wc, Wj) - Rd
Wc = Vcmax (Ci - Γ*) / [Ci + Kc(1 + O / Ko)]
Anet = [ (1/gbl) + (1/gs) ]⁻¹ · (Ca - Ci)

Biochemical carbon fixation analytically solved by matching RuBisCO/electron-transport capacity against boundary-layer (gbl) and stomatal (gs) diffusive supply, resolving exact intercellular CO₂ (Ci).

Aerodynamic Regimes: Forced Positive Pressure (Push) vs. Safe Bio-Containment Suction (Pull) In Push Mode, the external pump drives air into Port 1, creating positive chamber gauge pressure (+1.2 to +14.5 Pa), localized jet impingement, and recirculating corner eddies (γ ≈ 0.85–0.88). In Pull Mode, the pump pulls air out through Port 2, producing negative gauge pressure (−1.0 to −11.8 Pa). This suction mode provides intrinsic bio-containment for the crew cabin, eliminates air leakage risk, and achieves highly symmetric streamline convergence (γ > 0.95) across all three growth compartments.
  • 3. 5-Day Developmental Ontogeny & Canopy Drag Scaling Seeds germinate within 18–24 hours under capillary hydration.   • 4.
