Hardware & Fluidic Architecture

  • Mated CAD Envelope (Pot + Lid) Pot (`M09024F-MDL-5`): $47.63 \times 33.67 \times 67.95\ \text{mm}$. Lid (`M09025F-MDL-2`) rotated $90^\circ$ and seated at $Y = 33.67\ \text{mm}$ ($Y \in [32.40, 38.11]\ \text{mm}$).
  • 3-Part Partitioned Porous Substrate ($Y = 7.62\text{–}14.62\ \text{mm}$) Split into 3 discrete blocks seated on internal chamber retainer ledges ($\Delta Y = 7.00\ \text{mm}$, $\epsilon = 0.50$, $K = 10^{-10}\ \text{m}^2$). Total bulk volume $= 10.55\ \text{cm}^3$ ($5.28\ \text{mL}$ void volume).
  • Lower Aeration & Drainage Base ($Y = 0.0\text{–}7.62\ \text{mm}$) Molded basal troughs ($8.74\ \text{cm}^3$) below the substrate ledges provide root zone oxygenation and capillary moisture buffering.
  • Open Central Plenum ($\Delta Y = 14.2\ \text{mm}$ in Chamber 2) Because Chamber 2 houses prostrate Arabidopsis rosettes ($h = 4.9\ \text{mm}$) between tall B. rapa canopies ($h = 11.4\ \text{mm}$), air skims across Chamber 1, dips into Chamber 2's open plenum, converges with Chamber 3, and exits cleanly out Port 2.
  • Port 3 Sub-Surface Dipping Nozzle ($Y = 9.5\ \text{mm}$) Delivers water and harvest-phase RNAlater directly into the core of Substrate Block 3, driving bottom-up capillary imbibition without foliar splashing.
