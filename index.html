4. Mathematical Physics & CFD Transport Governing Equations

Full 3D Navier–Stokes, Brinkman–Darcy porous media drag, Churchill mixed convection, and coupled Farquhar–von Caemmerer–Berry (FvCB) photosynthesis formulations.

1. Incompressible Navier–Stokes with Brinkman Porous Drag & Boussinesq Buoyancy

∇ · u = 0
∂u/∂t + (u · ∇)u = -(1/ρ₀)∇p + ν∇²u - D(x)u + g[βT(T - T₀) + βH₂O(CH₂O - CH₂O,0) + βCO₂(CCO₂ - CCO₂,0)]

Continuity and momentum balance where ν = 1.51 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s. The momentum sink D(x) equals Darcy drag ν / (εK) inside the 3 partitioned substrate blocks (Y = 7.62–14.62 mm) and canopy drag Cd · aleaf(x) · |u| in foliar zones. Buoyancy acceleration drops 83.4% on the Moon (g = 1.62 m/s²).

2. Multi-Species Advection–Diffusion & Sandberg Residence-Time Identity

∂Ck/∂t + u · ∇Ck = Dk ∇²Ck + Sk(x)
u · ∇θ - Dθ ∇²θ = 1,   θ|Port 1 = 0
⟨θ⟩outlet, flux = ∫Port 2 (u · n)θ dA / ∫Port 2 (u · n) dA ≡ τnominal = Vfluid / Q

Scalar transport for CO₂ (D = 1.60 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s) and H₂O vapor (D = 2.42 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s). The Mean Age-of-Air (θ) PDE verifies ventilation efficiency, validated against the exact Sandberg conservation ratio (θout / τ = 0.938).

3. Churchill Combined Free-and-Forced Leaf Boundary Layer Conductance

δeff,c = (δforced,c⁻³ + δnat,c⁻³)-1/3
δforced,c = 4.92 √(ν Lleaf,c / |uc|),   δnat,c = 3.5 Lleaf,c Grc-1/4
gbl,c = DCO₂ / (δeff,c · Vmol)

Churchill asymptotic blending across laminar forced Blasius flat-plate boundary layers and natural Grashof buoyancy convection. Evaluated with Lleaf = 20 mm for B. rapa and 8 mm for Arabidopsis.

4. Coupled Farquhar–von Caemmerer–Berry (FvCB) Photosynthesis & Stomatal Supply

Anet = min(Wc, Wj) - Rd
Wc = Vcmax (Ci - Γ*) / [Ci + Kc(1 + O / Ko)]
Anet = [ (1/gbl) + (1/gs) ]⁻¹ · (Ca - Ci)

Biochemical carbon fixation analytically solved by matching RuBisCO/electron-transport capacity against boundary-layer (gbl) and stomatal (gs) diffusive supply, resolving exact intercellular CO₂ (Ci).

Model Lunar LEAF Experimental Multi-Physics Summary

  • 1. Initial Water Activation & Nytex Mesh Root Exclusion Barrier At mission commencement (Hour 0), 7.39 mL of deionized water is injected through Port 3 into the lower foam cassette, achieving 100% liquid saturation across all 3 partitioned foam blocks (φ = 0.70, total bulk volume 10.55 cm³). Wrapping the foam is a medical-grade 35 μm monofilament nylon (Nytex) screen with an 8.04 kPa Laplace bubble point. This ensures complete capillary moisture continuity to the seedbed while providing a 100% root exclusion margin against primary radicles (90–320 μm diameter), preventing root penetration and pore clogging in the foam matrix.
  • 2. Aerodynamic Regimes: Forced Positive Pressure (Push) vs. Safe Bio-Containment Suction (Pull) In Push Mode, the external pump drives air into Port 1, creating positive chamber gauge pressure (+1.2 to +14.5 Pa), localized jet impingement, and recirculating corner eddies (γ ≈ 0.85–0.88). In Pull Mode, the pump pulls air out through Port 2, producing negative gauge pressure (−1.0 to −11.8 Pa). This suction mode provides intrinsic bio-containment for the crew cabin, eliminates air leakage risk, and achieves highly symmetric streamline convergence (γ > 0.95) across all three growth compartments.
  • 3. 5-Day Developmental Ontogeny & Canopy Drag Scaling Seeds germinate within 18–24 hours under capillary hydration. Cotyledons emerge by Day 2 (Hour 48), marking the autotrophic transition where net carbon assimilation crosses from negative (respiration: −0.85 μmol/m²s) to positive photosynthetic uptake (+0.12 → +13.41 μmol/m²s in B. rapa). Over 120 hours, 164 seedlings transpire a cumulative 4.19 mL of water. At Day 5, B. rapa reaches 11.26 mm in height, while Arabidopsis forms a 4.75 mm rosette lawn, leaving a 14.2 mm clear open plenum in Chamber 2 for unimpeded flow convergence into Port 2.
  • 4. Environmental Multi-Physics Sweeps (Gravity 0–1g & Temperature 18–30 °C) Coupled Navier–Stokes and energy simulations across microgravity (0.0g), Lunar (0.166g), Martian (0.38g), and Earth (1.0g) confirm that forced convection dominates (Richardson number Ri ≤ 0.04) at nominal ventilation rates (Q = 0.50–0.85 L/min). Temperature sweeps prove that the optical lid condensation safety margin remains positive (ΔTmargin = +0.83 °C to +2.45 °C), guaranteeing clear, fog-free time-lapse imaging across all operational envelopes.
